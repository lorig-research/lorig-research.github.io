# Conference Presentation on Qiangic and Macro-Gyalrongic Languages

**18 August 2025**

On 18 August 2025, James Engels of the University of Edinburgh and Lo-Rig Principal Investigator Tim Bodt presented a paper titled **"Qiangic interrelatedness and Macro-Gyalrongic: new insights from Songlin"** at the **27th International Conference on Historical Linguistics** in Santiago de Chile, Chile.

The conference brought together around 500 historical linguists from around the world. The presentation contributed to ongoing discussions on the historical relationships among Trans-Himalayan languages, with particular attention to Songlin and its position in relation to Qiangic, Chamdoic, and Macro-Gyalrongic languages.

More information about the conference is available on the official conference website:
