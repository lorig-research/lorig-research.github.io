# Presentation at the 58th International Conference on Sino-Tibetan Languages and Linguistics

**04 September 2025**

On 04 September 2025, Lo-Rig Principal Investigator Tim Bodt presented on **"Contact between Kusunda and Sino-Tibetan"** at the **58th International Conference on Sino-Tibetan Languages and Linguistics (ICSTLL58)**, held at the Department of Linguistics, University of Bern, Switzerland.

The talk was attended by around 30 linguists working on the Sino-Tibetan (Trans-Himalayan, Tibeto-Burman) language family, to which Gongduk and Monkha also belong.

More information about ICSTLL58 is available on the University of Bern website:

[58th International Conference on Sino-Tibetan Languages and Linguistics](https://www.isw.unibe.ch/research/workshops/icstll_international_conference/index_eng.html)
